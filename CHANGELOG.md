# Changelog

All notable changes are recorded here. Versions follow semantic versioning
(LOF-P-006).

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-16

- Added `role modification act` (ODP002_0001004) and `modifies` (ODP002_0001104). W. Mandrick, ODP 2 slide and issue #8.
- A legal role now has exactly one bearer, as ISO/IEC 21838-2 requires. From W. Mandrick's change-of-command use case.
- Documented how conferral acts relate to time (BFO occupies temporal region), to organizations and their members (via CCO), and to the power they realize (via LOF-ODP-003 and D-Acts). Three new competency questions.
